## Fiat-Shamir Heuristic
Also known as Fiat-Shamir transformation, is a method that takes an interactive 
proof and turns it into a non-interactive proof and creates a digital signature. 
This transformation is covered in more detail in the page on <a href="../mpcith#fiat">MPC in the Head</a>.
